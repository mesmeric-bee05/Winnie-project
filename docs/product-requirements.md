# Product requirements

## Approved MVP

Winnie is a career-development platform that makes progression observable through practical work, guided milestones, mentor feedback, portfolio evidence and skill-specific endorsements.

The first pathway is intentionally role-agnostic in the demo layer. The client must approve the initial real role/pathway, brand name, legal entity, target market, user eligibility and employer data-sharing rules before production data is enabled.

## In scope

- public product narrative;
- mentee onboarding concepts;
- pathway and milestone model;
- practical task and evidence model;
- portfolio and endorsement model;
- reviewer workspace;
- consent-scoped employer preview;
- admin foundation.

## Deferred

Investor marketplace, automated payouts, provider revenue splitting, open employer search, algorithmic hiring decisions, full course marketplace, native mobile, complex video conferencing, and payments without approved pricing, tax, KYC/KYB, refunds and dispute rules.

# Incident response

1. Identify and contain the affected surface.
2. Preserve logs and timestamps without copying secrets or unnecessary personal data.
3. Revoke or rotate exposed credentials and sessions.
4. Assess affected records, users and third parties.
5. Notify the Client and relevant processors or authorities as required.
6. Patch, test and deploy the remediation.
7. Record root cause, lessons learned and prevention actions.

Emergency owner and legal/privacy contacts must be filled before production launch.
